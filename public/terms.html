Health Notice

The game contains flashing lights (muzzle flashes, hit effects) and fast motion. If you are sensitive to flashing images or prone to photosensitive seizures, play with caution or not at all. Violence is cartoon-level: cats keel over; there is no gore.

Leaderboard Fair Play

The leaderboard is for real runs. Submitting fabricated or automated scores, impersonating others via callsign, or using offensive callsigns isn't welcome — implausible submissions are rejected automatically, and we may remove any entry or wipe the board entirely to keep it honest. Scores are a public display, not a stored asset you own.
