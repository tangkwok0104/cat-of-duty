Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: 2026-08-12 · V1.2

The Short Version

Cat of Duty is a free browser game with no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Your settings and best score are stored only in your own browser, and the game sends nothing anywhere — with one opt-in exception: if you choose to post a score to the leaderboard, the callsign and run stats you see on screen are sent to the board (details below). Nothing is ever sent without you pressing the button.

What the Game Stores

The game keeps exactly two pieces of data, both in your browser's localStorage, both only on your device:

  • cod-settings — your mouse sensitivity, field of view, quality tier, and volume choices.
  • cod-best — your best score.

Neither is transmitted to us or anyone else. There are no cookies, no tracking pixels, no analytics scripts, and no third-party embeds. Because this storage is strictly functional (it exists so your settings survive a reload), no consent banner is required — and we don't show one.

Hosting

The game's files are served by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel processes standard technical request data (such as your IP address and browser user agent) to deliver the site and keep it secure. That processing is governed by Vercel's privacy policy. We do not receive, store, or analyze that data ourselves.

Field Reports (Feedback)

The optional FIELD REPORT button composes a bug report and hands it to GitHub Issues in a new tab. Nothing is sent until you submit it there yourself, and anything you submit becomes a public GitHub issue under GitHub's privacy statement. The report includes technical details shown to you before you send it (game version, wave reached, quality tier, browser user agent, viewport size) — review and edit freely before submitting.

Leaderboard (Opt-In)

Posting a score is optional and manual — the POST SCORE button on the mission-failed screen. When you press it, we store: your chosen callsign, the run's score, wave, kills, accuracy and duration, and a random per-browser id (used only to highlight your own rows). Callsigns and scores are shown publicly on the in-game board, so don't use your real name as a callsign. The board is hosted on Supabase. To rate-limit abuse, a salted hash of your IP address is kept for up to two hours around a submission, then deleted; the IP itself is never stored with your score. To remove a score, open a GitHub issue naming the callsign and we'll delete it.

Friend Rooms (Opt-In)

If you create or join a friend room, your callsign and your live run status (wave, score, health, alive/down) are shared with the one person in your room, relayed through Supabase's realtime service while the room is open. Nothing is recorded: rooms have no database — they exist only as live connections and vanish when you leave. Anyone you give the room code or link can join your room, so share it only with people you actually want to play with.

Your Rights & Deleting Your Data

Because your data never leaves your browser, you are already in full control of it. To delete everything the game stores, clear this site's data in your browser settings (or clear localStorage for this site). There is nothing on our side to request, export, or erase — under GDPR, CCPA, or any similar framework, we simply hold no personal data about you.

Changes & Contact

If the game ever starts collecting anything new (for example, an online leaderboard), this policy will be updated first and the "Last Updated" date above will change. Questions or concerns: open an issue on GitHub.
