# Asset licensing

**The PolyForm Noncommercial license in `LICENSE` covers the source code only.
It does not grant any rights to the art, audio, or 3D assets in
`public/assets/`.** Those are addressed here.

Note that the code license is already noncommercial — nothing in this file
grants commercial rights to anything either.

Those assets fall into two groups. Per-file provenance for every one of them is
recorded in [`CREDITS.md`](./CREDITS.md).

## 1. Third-party CC0 assets — free to reuse

The PBR texture sets and the HDRI environment come from
[Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) and are published under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain).
You may reuse these freely; attribution is appreciated but not required.

- `public/assets/hdri/` — Poly Haven HDRI
- `public/assets/textures/` — Poly Haven PBR texture sets

## 2. Generated assets — rights reserved

Everything under `public/assets/gen/` (character and weapon models, animation
clips, sound effects, posters, menu key art, logo) was generated on the
project owner's own paid accounts with third-party AI tools — Higgsfield
(Recraft, Nano Banana, Mirelo) and Meshy.

These are **not** covered by the code license and are **not** offered for reuse.
They ship in this repository so the game runs when you clone it, nothing more.
Redistribution of these files, on their own or as part of another project, is
not permitted here. If you fork the project, please substitute your own assets.

The reason for the reservation is honesty rather than restriction: the
generating platforms' terms govern what may be sub-licensed onward, and the
owner has not audited those terms. Reserving rights is the conservative
position until that check happens.

## Nothing here comes from a commercial game

No asset, sound, HUD layout, font, or line of text was copied from Call of
Duty, PUBG, or any other commercial game. The visual and audio design is
original or CC0 throughout. See the note in [`README.md`](./README.md) about
the project's parody name.
